Our story

We started in 2019 after a frustrating month of chasing certificates for a compound we had already bought. Half the lots arrived with no documentation. The other half had documents that did not match the vial. So we built the supplier we wanted: a narrow catalogue, a COA on every batch, and a person who answers the phone.

What we actually do

We buy from ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturers, then send each lot to an independent US analytical laboratory. Purity is measured by HPLC-UV, identity by LC-MS. Endotoxins, microbial contamination and heavy metals are screened on the compounds where it matters. Results are published against the lot number printed on your vial.

  • One batch record per lot, archived for five years.
  • Net compound content reported, not estimated.
  • Insulated, shock-absorbing packaging on every shipment.
  • No account required to order, and no marketing calls afterwards.

Who we serve

University labs, teaching hospitals, contract research organisations, and secondary-school science departments. Institutions can order against a purchase order and receive an invoice net 30. We do not sell to the general public, and every order is confirmed as research use only.

Where we are

Our warehouse is in Columbus, Ohio. Orders placed before 2pm ET leave the same day and arrive in two to three business days. Support runs Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm ET.
