# E-commerce UX

From Baymard Institute research on why people abandon carts.

- **Guest checkout:** never force people to create an account to buy. Forced accounts are
  one of the top reasons people abandon carts.
- **Short checkout form:** ask only for what's needed. The average checkout has about 15
  fields, roughly double what most purchases need.
- **Show the total cost early:** subtotal, shipping, and tax are visible in the cart
  *before* checkout. Surprise costs are the #1 reason people abandon carts.
- **Clear error messages:** say exactly what's wrong and how to fix it, next to the field.
- **Product pages:** big clear images, price, stock status, specs, and an obvious "Add to
  cart" button.
- **Search and filters:** search finds products by name and category. Always show a
  helpful "no results" message.
- Show trust signals: contact info, return policy, secure-checkout note.
- After "Add to cart", give clear feedback (the cart count updates and a short message
  appears).
