# Workflow

How to approach any task in this project.

## Before every task

1. Read `../../Aboutme.md` to understand who I am.
2. Read `../../brand/brand-guide.md` before any design or writing work.
3. Read `plan.md` and follow it. Build in the order it lists.
4. Read `progress.md` to see what's done and what's next.

## Execute, don't stall

- When given a plan or an instruction, **execute it. Start building right away.**
- Don't ask "should I proceed?" or "is this ok?". Just do the task.
- If something is unclear, make the sensible choice, say what you chose, and keep going.
- The only times to stop and ask: deleting or replacing files, or when an instruction
  contradicts `plan.md`.

## Progress

- After each step, update `progress.md`: move finished items to **Done** and update
  **Next**.

## Safety

- Ask me before deleting or replacing any file.
- Change only what the task asks for.
