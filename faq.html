Shipping & delivery

How fast do orders ship?

Orders placed before 2pm ET leave our Columbus warehouse the same business day. Weekend orders ship on Monday. Delivery is two to three business days within the US, tracked door to door.

What does shipping cost?

Flat $12 for orders under $199. Free on everything above $199. The cost is shown in the cart before you start checkout, never added at the end.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. We currently fulfil US addresses only, which keeps transit time and temperature exposure predictable.

How are compounds packed?

Vials travel in moulded inserts inside an insulated outer carton. Glassware gets an extra shock layer. Cold-chain options are available on request for larger institutional orders.

Testing & certificates of analysis

What is tested on each batch?

Purity by HPLC-UV, identity by LC-MS, and net compound content. Where relevant we also screen endotoxins (USP <85> LAL), microbial contamination by PCR, and heavy metals by ICP-MS. The measured values are printed on the certificate, not just a pass or fail.

How do I find the COA for my vial?

Every vial carries a lot number. Email that number to hello@labsupply.example and we send the matching certificate the same business day, or browse the COA library linked from any product page.

Who does the testing?

An independent US analytical laboratory. We do not test in-house, so the result is not self-certified.

How long are records kept?

Batch files stay archived for five years, so a re-order in 2029 still points to the original test data.

Returns

Unopened, undamaged product can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Send the lot number first so we can check the batch record. Opened vials cannot be returned for safety reasons. If something arrives damaged, photograph the parcel and we replace it at our cost.

Ordering for schools and institutions

We accept purchase orders from accredited schools, universities and research institutions, invoiced net 30. Add your PO number in the order notes at checkout, or email a copy to hello@labsupply.example and we will match it to the order.

Research-use-only policy

All products are supplied for laboratory research and educational use only. They are not for human or animal consumption, medical use, veterinary use, or any therapeutic, diagnostic or clinical purpose. By ordering you confirm you are a qualified researcher or institution, that you will handle the materials under appropriate safety protocols, and that you will not resell or transfer them.

We cannot provide dosing, administration or human-use guidance of any kind. Orders that suggest otherwise may be refused.
