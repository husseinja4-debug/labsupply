Testing methodology

Four tests, every batch, every time.

LabSupply sends representative samples from every manufacturing batch to an independent, US-based analytical laboratory before release. No batch ships without a signed Certificate of Analysis.

  1. 01

    Identity

    Mass spectrometry [M+H]+ confirms molecular weight matches theoretical value.

  2. 02

    Purity

    Reverse-phase HPLC with UV detection quantifies compound content and impurities.

  3. 03

    Endotoxin

    Two replicate LAL endotoxin screening, with a documented pass or fail.

  4. 04

    Appearance

    Visual and quantitative verification of lyophilised product weight.
