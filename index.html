Batch records

Four measurements per lot. Published, not paraphrased.

A representative sample is drawn from every manufactured batch and tested by an independent US laboratory before release. No batch ships without a signed certificate of analysis.

Read the testing policy
  1. 01

    Identity

    Mass spectrometry [M+H]+ compared with the theoretical value.

  2. 02

    Purity

    Reverse-phase HPLC with UV detection, reported to two decimals.

  3. 03

    Endotoxin

    Two LAL replicates, with a documented pass or fail.

  4. 04

    Appearance

    Visual check and net fill weight per vial.
